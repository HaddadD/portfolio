Scotiabank EITS Chatbots — CLOUDia & STAX

Exploring how chatbot branding and design impact the employee self-help experience.

RoleUX Design & Research
Team12 people
DurationSummer co-op, through project evolution

Overview

Scotiabank's Employee IT Services (EITS) department offered three ways for staff to get help: knowledge bases, chatbots, and service desk support. The goal of this project was creating a unified portal — the Scotiabank Technology Help Centre.

Problem Identification

We discovered that roughly 50% of all calls to the service desk were marked as simple solutions, which led to the core problem statement:

How might we reduce the number of simple solutions reaching our service desk?

Design Evolution

Phase 1 — CLOUDia

After a competitive analysis of banking and tech company solutions, the team pursued chatbot development. I contributed avatar redesigns and improved chat experience features, including back buttons and answer-choice buttons rather than free text input. A style guide was created for the gendered avatar.

Phase 2 — STAX

After 18 months, Scotiabank's gender compliance team required a redesign. The rebranded chatbot — STAX (Scotiabank's Technology Assistance eXpert) — adopted a gender-neutral approach, with staff involved in naming through a workplace poll.
