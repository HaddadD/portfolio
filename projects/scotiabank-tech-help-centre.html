Scotiabank Technology Help Centre

Consolidating three support channels into one unified self-help portal for employees.

Overview

Scotiabank's Employee IT Services offered employees three separate ways to get technical support: a knowledge base, a chatbot, and the service desk. This project consolidated all three into one unified portal — the Scotiabank Technology Help Centre.

RoleUX Design & Research
Team12 people
Timeline2020–2021

Research Findings

A survey of employees revealed two key insights:

63%
Contact the service desk first
70%
Unaware of other support methods available

This pattern created bottlenecks, overloading the service desk with issues that could often be resolved through self-help.

Problem Statement

How might we better improve the self-help strategy for our users?

Pain Points

  1. Excessive clicks needed to locate the right resource
  2. Unclear article content and presentation
  3. Low awareness of available support channels

User Personas

  • Juan — a new hire seeking guidance on how to get help
  • Cathy — a long-term employee adjusting to remote work

Testing & Results

We ran usability testing with 23 employees across 7 scenarios (4 per participant), tracking each as successful, semi-successful, or unsuccessful to identify where the experience broke down.

Solution

The result was an enhanced homepage search experience — enabling content-based searches across both articles and forms, with filtering to help employees find the right resource in fewer clicks and raise overall awareness of self-help options.
