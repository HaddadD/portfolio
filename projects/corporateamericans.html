The Corporate Americans

1,000 unique NFT characters representing the diversity of America's working class.

Project Overview

The Corporate Americans is a collection of 1,000 unique NFT characters, combining various data sources to visually represent the unique characteristics of America's working-class population.

Motivation

Most popular NFT collections at the time were expensive and celebrity-driven. I wanted to create something relatable — a figure that was representative of you: someone working the regular 9-to-5.

Research & Development

  • Consolidated demographic data from multiple sources
  • Tracked characteristics: race, gender, sexual orientation, family status, number of children, and occupation
  • Distributed those percentages across 1,000 hand-designed characters
  • Created custom hairstyles, outfits, and color palettes by occupation

Production Process

  • Assembled characters in batches of 100 using a layer-based system
  • Used an automated macro program to upload NFTs to OpenSea (a 3-day process)
  • Promoted the collection via TikTok and Instagram Reels

Results

Commercial results were modest, but the project was a huge learning experience — and one I'm proud of artistically.
