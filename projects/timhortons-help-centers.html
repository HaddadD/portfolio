Tim Hortons Help Centers & TimBot

A centralized, AI-assisted support hub for Guests.

Overview

A centralized, AI-assisted support hub for Guests across Tim Hortons and Tims Financial — consolidating fragmented support content into unified Help Centres and introducing TimBot, an AI chatbot that answers questions conversationally.

RoleLead Designer & Illustrator
Team1 Designer, 3 Customer Support Reps, 2 Legal team, and 1 Copywriter
Timeline2024–2025

The Challenge

This project covered two major initiatives:

  • Designing the Tims Financial Support Centre — the inaugural help resource for the new Tims Mastercard product, with no existing templates to build from
  • Reimagining the Tim Hortons Help Centre to consolidate fragmented support information across nine product categories into one unified system

Research Findings

User research revealed two critical priorities:

  • A prominent search function as the primary element on the page
  • A smart chatbot — one that could understand natural language, guide users contextually, and escalate complex issues to live support when needed

Design Approach

I used Figma for wireframing and selected Zendesk's Hudson Theme as the platform foundation, customizing it with branded heading fields and flexible content blocks.

Key Features

Tim Hortons Help Centre

  • Scalable architecture supporting 400+ articles
  • Smart ticket routing distinguishing in-app from in-restaurant issues
  • Automatic localization for Quebec and U.S. users
  • Cross-linking to Tims Financial resources

TimBot Chatbot

Powered by AI, TimBot scans Help Centre articles to deliver summarized answers in real time — letting Guests solve problems conversationally or escalate when necessary.

Tims Financial Support Centre

  • Search bar positioned prominently
  • Dynamic homepage featuring ~100 articles
  • Data-driven content curation based on analytics
  • Persistent support contact link

Results

86%
Tims Financial articles rated 75%+ helpful
−44%
Support tickets post-launch

Reflection

A natural next step is connecting chatbot analytics to article feedback — proactively updating content based on AI-detected friction points.
