Tims Mastercard Onboarding

A holistic redesign of the credit card onboarding flow.

Overview

A holistic, end-to-end redesign of the Tims Mastercard onboarding experience. By mapping the entire journey rather than optimizing isolated screens, we cut the flow from 18 steps to 11 and grew monthly signups by 24% month over month.

RoleLead Designer
Team3 Product Managers, 1 Customer Support Rep, 2 Legal Team Members, 1 Product Analytics, 3 Developers and 1 Copywriter
Timeline2023–2024

The Challenge

We identified high abandonment rates across multiple stages of the Tims Mastercard onboarding flow. Rather than optimizing individual high-abandonment pages in isolation, I advocated for a comprehensive end-to-end review. A cross-functional workshop involving Design, Product, Development, Legal, Product Analytics, and Customer Support teams mapped the entire experience and identified friction points.

Research Methodology

I created visual "Lego Blocks" — color-coded representations showing abandonment data per screen. This tool helped the team see exactly where users dropped off and which steps felt unnecessary or intimidating.

Problem Statement

We needed a unified, bilingual, scalable onboarding flow that reduced drop-off and delivered a consistent, trustworthy brand experience.

The Process

Thirteen key improvements were identified, including:

  1. Clarify Product Message — Replaced the carousel with a dedicated marketing page
  2. Simplify Legal Agreements — Consolidated four consent checkboxes into one
  3. Remove Credit Confusion — Automated eligibility evaluation behind the scenes
  4. Quebec Compliance — Leveraged language preferences to eliminate a redundant step
  5. Merge Screens — Combined back-to-back information collection (name, DOB, address, employment)
  6. Humanize Copy — Rewrote legal/verification language into accessible explanations
  7. Remove Duplicate Confirmation — Eliminated a redundant shipping address screen
  8. Celebration Redesign — Made the approval screen more encouraging
  9. Maintain Momentum — Ensured CTA consistency across post-approval steps
  10. Unify Visual Design — Removed distracting steppers and oversized headers
